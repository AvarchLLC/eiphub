A collaborative space at Devcon 8 India dedicated to Ethereum Improvement Proposals, ERC standards, protocol upgrades, and standards adoption. It brings together contributors from across the Ethereum ecosystem for technical discussions, office hours, and community engagement.
Anyone interested in shaping Ethereum: first-time EIP authors, protocol researchers, client developers, ERC authors, wallet developers, infrastructure teams, enterprise architects, educators, students, and open-source maintainers. Special focus on contributors from India and the wider APAC region.
3–6 November 2026, inside the Jio World Centre, Mumbai — the Devcon 8 India venue. A Devcon ticket is required to access the space. Join on Luma to receive location details and updates as they are confirmed.
The full schedule is on Luma at lu.ma/czi58jpe. Join there and you will get session reminders and agenda updates directly in your calendar.
Selected sessions will be livestreamed and recorded. If you cannot join in person, you can watch recordings afterward.
